# ICPC 2025

First competitive programming contest. Went in not really knowing what to expect.

I took the easier end of the problem set — solved Snakey String and Memories of Passport Control, which let my teammates focus on harder stuff without having to come back and clean up. That part of the strategy worked. What didn't was problem queue management — nobody was tracking how long we'd been on something, and toward the end we had overlap and wasted time we could've spent elsewhere.

Spent the back half of the contest stuck on "Much Room for Mushrooms." Had the right shape of the solution — something involving even/odd parity and combinatorial counting — but couldn't turn the intuition into actual math. The logic was there. The formulas weren't. Ended up spinning.

## Where the gaps are

Combinatorics, closed-form derivation, parity tricks. I can reason through problems but I don't have the standard formulas memorized, so I'm re-deriving from scratch under time pressure. Also need to practice standard algorithms (DP, graph traversal) until implementation is automatic, not something I have to figure out mid-contest.

Next time someone needs to own the queue and call time when a problem has stalled long enough.
